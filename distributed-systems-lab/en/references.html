Reading and experiment boundaries

The course covers crash/recovery coordination, a single consensus group, completed single-register histories and a deliberately small downstream fence. It does not teach all of distributed systems: sharding, BFT, CRDT implementation, distributed garbage collection, production authentication, packet fault matrices and verified disaster restore remain extensions. No learning-effect measurement has been performed.

The real-run report is generated from the selected assertions, with hashes for integrity. Timings are observations, not benchmarks with statistical power. The search checker can return inconclusive and does not consume unknown-outcome calls.
