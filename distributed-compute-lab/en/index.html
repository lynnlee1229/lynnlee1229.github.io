Enter at the right depth

New to distributed computing? Know loops, functions, key/value pairs and basic sums; read Setup, then Chapters 01–07 in order. Already use an engine? Begin with the prediction and exit task of each chapter, inspect the saved plan, then reproduce before skipping ahead. No domain-specific experience is required.
